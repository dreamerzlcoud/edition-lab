Data & method

Where the sales came from

StockX sampled a fixed, undisclosed share of U.S. Off-White × Nike and Yeezy 350 resales per colorway and day. Of 99,956 trades from September 2017 to February 2019, 98,705 fall in 75 complete Monday–Sunday weeks.

StockX data release · Original workbook · Weekly counts (CSV)

How we tested the models

After 63 weeks of history, each model predicts the next week using earlier sales. The baseline averages the last four weeks. The regression fits an intercept, last week’s sales and the four-week mean, then clips negative predictions to zero. Both update weekly.

The chart compares predictions with the following 12 actual counts. Mean absolute error averages the size of those misses before rounding.

How the game works

The game draws from 33 earlier weeks. Each forecast uses only sales known before its round. Stock is hypothetical, with one interchangeable pair per sampled sale and no replenishment. The gap between your stock and observed sales determines the result.

StockX provides no factory output, stockout records or counts of shoppers who could not buy. These quantities illustrate decisions at the scale of the sample.
